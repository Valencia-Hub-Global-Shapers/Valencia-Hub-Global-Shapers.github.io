Drop real team photos here, named to match the `photo` path in `data/team.json`
(e.g. `laura-ferrer.jpg`). Until a photo exists at that path, the site shows
an automatic initials avatar instead — nothing breaks if this folder stays
empty during development.

Recommended: square or 4:3 photos, at least 600px wide, under ~300KB each.

## Site photos (not here — in `assets/img/`)

Two full-bleed photos live in `assets/img/`, not this folder:

- `hub-hero.jpg` — homepage hero band. A wide landscape / aerial group
  shot works best; shown up to 520px tall, cropped `center 42%`.
- `Team-Photo.jpeg` — photo band under the Team page header. Landscape,
  shown up to 480px tall, cropped `center 38%`.

If either file is missing the band is simply hidden — nothing breaks.
