What the hub is

A civic engine run by Valencia's own young people

The Valencia Hub is part of the Global Shapers Community, an initiative of the World Economic Forum bringing together people aged 18–27 who are already active in their cities. Every member volunteers their time alongside their studies or job.

We choose local problems — housing, climate, mobility, education — and design projects with the people affected by them, using the training, network, and visibility the global community provides.

Volunteer-run

No one draws a salary. Every hour comes from members' own time.

Youth-led

Every Shaper is between 18 and 27 and leads their own projects.

Local impact

Projects are designed with and for Valencia's neighbourhoods.

Global network

Backed by hubs and resources in over 150 countries.

Our connection to the World Economic Forum

One thread, from Geneva to your street

The Valencia Hub is an officially recognised chapter of the Global Shapers Community, the World Economic Forum's youth initiative. That affiliation gives us training, funding opportunities, and a seat at conversations that shape global policy — which we bring straight back to Valencia.
